Recycle North Geelong & Australian Paper Recovery: Leading Change

In Greater Geelong, Recycle North Geelong plays a key role. Operating an all‑weather, three‑acre undercover facility in North Geelong, it serves both households and businesses by providing drop‑off and transfer station services throughout the week  . Their focus extends beyond household recycling—they accept large and commercial volumes of clean, flattened cardboard and redirect these materials to Australian Paper Recovery.

Australian Paper Recovery offers large‑scale waste paper and cardboard recovery solutions. They supply commercial compactors, balers and collection bins, helping businesses and centres handle cardboard effectively and ensuring it enters Australia’s local recycling stream rather than being exported or landfilled  .

Through this coordinated system, tens of tonnes of cardboard are processed every week that historically would have been landfilled. While exact tonnages at Recycle North Geelong are not publicly published, significant local diversion is evident: with business and residential users dropping off weekly, the facility helps shift a major proportion of commercial cardboard waste away from landfill.

Why This Matters

  • Nationwide, paper and cardboard recovery rates sit at about 65% overall, but local programs like this help push those recovery rates up in the commercial stream which accounts for a huge part of national waste  .
  • For every tonne of cardboard recycled, up to 17 trees could be saved, and around 9,000 litres of water conserved  .
  • Australian Paper Recovery aims toward zero waste to landfill, and by partnering with regional facilities like Recycle North Geelong, both residential and commercial cardboard divert operations support a circular economy locally and statewide  .

In summary, cardboard waste remains a major environmental issue in Australia, with millions of tonnes generated every year and too much still destined for landfill. Recycle North Geelong—and its collaboration with Australian Paper Recovery—demonstrates a successful regional model: diverting tens of tonnes weekly, recovering valuable material, reducing greenhouse gas emissions, and helping Australia reclaim and recycle what was once waste. This grassroots initiative shows how local partnerships can deliver big environmental benefits.
