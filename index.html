Before you come

Quick answers

Do I need to sort my load?

You'll pay about half if you do. A sorted ute load is $99; the same load unsorted is $199. Mixed construction and demolition waste isn't accepted at all.

What can go in an unsorted load?

Mixed household junk: mattresses, whitegoods, clothing and homewares, general household rubbish, furniture and books. Green waste is accepted in utes, trailers and tip trucks.

Are you open on Sundays?

From 1 September to 31 March, Sundays are 9am–1pm. From 1 April to 31 August the site is closed on Sundays.

Do you take asbestos or chemicals?

No. Asbestos, hazardous chemicals, solvents, fuels, pesticides, contaminated soil, medical waste and unlabelled chemicals are not accepted.

What happens to what I drop off?

It's sorted into dedicated streams for recycling under a 100% landfill-free commitment. Reusable items go to local charities and housing services through the Freecycle program.
