      05
      After you tip

      Landfill buries it.We take it apart.

      Scroll to follow five things that come through the gate every day.

      You drop off a mattress

      It leaves as baled steel

      Every mattress is dismantled on site, in about 38 seconds on average. The springs are pulled out, compressed and baled.

      99%of the steel recovered. About 30% of a mattress is steel.
      You drop off polystyrene

      It leaves as a dense block

      Polystyrene is up to 98% air. Our densifier presses it out, so three shipping containers of foam become one block about the size of a washing machine.

      98%air, pressed out on site
      You drop off cardboard

      It leaves as a bale

      Clean, dry cardboard is flattened and baled on site, then sent for paper-making.

      Freefor up to 0.5 m³ of clean cardboard
      You drop off a good couch

      It goes to someone who needs it

      Furniture in good condition goes to charity and community partners through our Freecycle program, helping people set up a home again.

      45+charity and community organisations we supply

      06
      How a visit works

      Four steps.That's it.

      Come in any time we're open: Monday to Saturday, 7:30am – 5:00pm. Rain or shine, it's all under cover.

      01

      Sort it at home

      Keep green waste, metal, e-waste and general rubbish apart as you load. It's quicker at the bays and halves the rate.

      02

      Drive in under cover

      Head to 116 Furner Avenue, behind Coates Hire. Follow the hanging signs to the right bay.

      03

      Unload by material

      Each material has its own bay, cage or tank. Not sure where something goes? Ask the team on the floor.

      04

      We take it from there

      90+ material streams, each with somewhere to go that isn't landfill.
