Before you come

Quick answers

Do I need to sort my load?

You'll pay about half if you do. A sorted ute load is $99; the same load unsorted is $199. Vans, trailers and trucks are $99 per m³ sorted and $199 per m³ unsorted. Mixed construction and demolition waste isn't accepted at all.

What can go in an unsorted load?

Mixed household junk: mattresses, whitegoods, clothing and homewares, general household rubbish, furniture and books. Green waste is accepted in utes, trailers and tip trucks.

How do I get the 15% trade discount?

Register a free trade and commercial account online with your name, business name, ABN and contact details. Residents in the local council areas can register for 10% off.

Are you open on Sundays?

From 1 September to 31 March, Sundays are 9am–1pm. From 1 April to 31 August the site is closed on Sundays and open Monday to Saturday, 7:30am–4pm.

Can I tip in my truck?

Yes, if it's 4,500kg GVM or under. Cars, wagons, utes, single and dual-axle trailers, tipping trailers, vans, and tip or box trucks up to 4,500kg GVM are all accepted. Trucks over 4,500kg GVM, skip bin trucks and garbage trucks can't tip here. Semi-trailers and B-Doubles are for corporate deliveries only, so call Nigel Taylor on 0427 888 222 first.

What's free to drop off?

Whitegoods, clean scrap metal, car batteries, printer cartridges, phones, TVs and LED screens, plus clean cardboard and EPS polystyrene up to 0.5 m³ each and up to 100 litres of architectural paint through PaintBack.

Do you take asbestos or chemicals?

No. Asbestos, hazardous chemicals, solvents, fuels, pesticides and herbicides, contaminated soil, medical and radioactive waste, unlabelled chemicals, weapons and mercury switches are not accepted. Neither are mixed construction and demolition loads.

Do you handle corporate e-waste?

Yes. E-waste is processed to AS 5377, hard drives are destroyed or sanitised to NIST 800-88, and Certificates of Destruction are supplied. Palletised loads are unloaded by forklift. Call Nigel Taylor on 0427 888 222.

What happens to what I drop off?

It's sorted into dedicated streams for recycling under a 100% landfill-free commitment. Reusable items go to local charities and housing services through the Freecycle program.
